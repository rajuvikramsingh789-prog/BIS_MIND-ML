🏛️ What is the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)?

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is the National Standards Body of India established under the BIS Act 2016. BIS is responsible for the harmonious development of standardization, marking, and quality certification of goods across India.

BIS operates major national initiatives including the flagship ISI Mark (Scheme I), Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) for electronics, Hallmarking of Gold and Silver Jewellery (IS 1417 / IS 15820), and manages the National Laboratory Recognition Scheme (LRS).

💡 Purpose of Know Your BIS

Navigating thousands of Indian Standards, Gazette Quality Control Orders (QCOs), testing protocols, and certification portals can be challenging for manufacturers, importers, small businesses, and consumers.

Know Your BIS bridges this gap by providing an instant, natural-language AI assistant and product-to-standard recommendation engine grounded exclusively in official BIS documents.
